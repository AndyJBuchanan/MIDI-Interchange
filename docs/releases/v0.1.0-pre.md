**First preview of MIDI Interchange**: a small desktop app for collecting MIDI from different music apps, choosing the parts you want and exporting them as one clean MIDI file.

It isn't a DAW: there's no playback, no instruments and no note editing. This is an early pre-release, so please report anything odd.

Intel Macs and macOS versions older than 15 aren't supported in this build.

## What you can do

- **Bring in MIDI:** drag MIDI clips straight from your music apps, or drag `.mid` files and folders onto the window. You can also use **File ▸ Open**, or (Windows) right-click a MIDI file ▸ **Open with**. Each import adds tracks to the workspace.
- **See your music:** every track appears as a lane on a timeline with its notes, lyrics, markers, and tempo and time signature changes.
- **Choose tracks:** untick tracks to leave them out of the export. You can also rename, recolour, reorder (drag ⋮⋮) and remove tracks.
- **Choose time ranges:**
  - Drag across the timeline to mark a range. Drag its edges to resize it, or drag it along the range strip to move it.
  - You can have several named ranges, and type exact positions as bar.beat.tick.
  - Snapping can be set to bar, beat or finer. Hold **Shift** to turn it off while dragging.
- **Export:** **Save MIDI…** writes one multitrack MIDI file. You can instead drag the **Drag MIDI out** tile into another app or a folder. Export options:
  - keep the ranges at their original positions
  - join the ranges back to back
  - save each range as its own file
  - trim the silence at the start and end
- **Safe edits:** your original files are never changed, and undo/redo covers everything.

Notes that cross a range boundary are cut cleanly, so no notes get stuck. Instrument and controller settings from before a range are carried into it. Lyrics, markers and other MIDI data are kept.

## Known limitations

- **Drag and drop with other apps depends on what each app supports.** On macOS it has been tried with Cubase (both ways), Reason (into Reason; Reason itself can't drag MIDI out), Staccato.ai and Scaler 3, and it works as far as each app allows. Other apps and Windows haven't been checked yet. If a drop doesn't work, the app says so and lists what the other app offered, which helps a lot in a bug report. Saving a MIDI file from your app and opening it always works.
- On Windows, opening several files at once from Explorer opens several windows. Drag them onto one window instead.
- Workspaces can't be saved or reopened yet.
- No app icon yet.

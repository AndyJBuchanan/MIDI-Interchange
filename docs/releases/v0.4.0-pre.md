**MIDI Interchange 0.4.0 (pre-release)**: a MIDI palette for moving MIDI between music apps. Collect clips, shape them with trims and splits, choose tracks and ranges, then drag or export the result to another app.

This release can open **Cubase 15 and 16** and **Reason 13 and 14** projects directly (other versions aren't supported yet). Drop a saved project on the window and its whole arrangement arrives as MIDI, with no export step in the DAW.

MIDI Interchange has no playback, instruments or note editing, and never changes your source files.

## What's new since 0.3.0

**Open DAW projects directly (experimental).**

- Drop a `.cpr` (Cubase 15 and 16), `.reason` (Reason 13 and 14) or `.dawproject` file, or open it from the toolbar. Each track arrives with its clips at their real positions, plus the tempo map, time signatures, markers, mutes and controller data. The project is only ever read, so it's safe with a project that is open in the DAW.
- **Cubase:** MIDI parts with trims, names, shared copies, part and track mute and solo; notes, controllers, pitch bend and program changes; tempo (ramps become a step every beat, as Cubase's own export does). Notes muted in Cubase's editors are left out, and the track transpose is applied.
- **Reason:** each track's note lanes as one track, holding exactly what Reason plays: loops written out, overlapped parts left out, Blocks written out at each placement. Notes, mod wheel, pitch bend (curves too), sustain and other controllers, tempo and time signatures. Muted clips, lanes and Block placements arrive as muted clips.
- **DAWproject:** arrangement MIDI, clip positions and mutes, loops, stepped tempo changes, time signatures and markers, from Bitwig, Studio One, Cubase and others.
- Checked against each DAW's own MIDI export of the same projects: on the real projects tested, Cubase and Reason match that export note for note, tempo map included.
- **Source Info** (right-click a track) shows the format and version and lists anything the import left out.

**Muted clips.**

- Muted clips stay on the timeline, drawn with thin crosses, and are left out of exports.
- Right-click a clip ▸ **Mute Clip** / **Unmute Clip**, or press **M** over it. This works on any clip. Mute state is kept in saved sessions and is undoable.

**Zoom to fit.** Press **Z** (or Ctrl/⌘+0) to fit the whole timeline. Zoom-to-fit now works on long timelines and after dropping several files at once, and you can zoom out much further.

**Smaller changes.**

- Dropping several files at once (End to End) keeps working with project files as well as MIDI.
- The Source Info window no longer grows a horizontal scrollbar on Windows.

## Verification

Automated tests cover the three readers, including note-for-note comparison against each DAW's MIDI export for the checked projects, mute handling, session upgrade and undo. The manual checklist was worked through for Cubase and Reason imports on macOS, and the Windows build was tested before release.

Built from commit `COMMIT`.

## Known limitations

- **Experimental readers.** The project formats aren't published, so the readers were built by studying saved projects. Other Cubase versions are refused with a clear message (Cubase 15 and 16 only); songs saved by Reason 12 and earlier are encrypted, so open them in Reason 13 or later and save again. A DAW update could change a format. If a project won't open, or something looks wrong, a copy of the project helps a lot in a bug report.
- **MIDI only.** Audio, instruments, plug-ins and device automation aren't imported, nor anything a DAW generates at playback: Cubase Pattern Editor parts, the chord track, MIDI inserts and expression-map articulations, and Reason pattern and Player devices. These are listed in Source Info.
- **Approximations.** Cubase folders are flattened; Reason's mixer mute and solo are not read; Reason clips' hidden notes and loop structure can't be brought back once written out. In the strongest Reason curves, the final piece of a steep curve can differ slightly from Reason's own export.
- **Reason automation lane:** importing channel-overridden MIDI can add an unwanted Controller #254 lane. The override works; delete the lane in Reason.
- Cursor switching occasionally stops working; the cause is unknown.
- On macOS, Esc cancellation of incoming Finder/Cubase drags remains unresolved. Moving the pointer outside the window restores the timeline view.
- Drag-and-drop support depends on the other app. Cubase drags one clip at a time; Logic can't drag MIDI out. Some apps don't adopt tempo from a drag; open the saved MIDI file instead.
- "Open With" is registered for MIDI files only; drag project files onto the window instead.
- There is no project Save/Open interface; session persistence keeps the current workspace automatically.
